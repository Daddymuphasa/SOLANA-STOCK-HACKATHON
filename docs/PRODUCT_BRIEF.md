# Stockana Product Brief

## One-liner

Stockana is a PreStocks research and portfolio simulation workspace that helps Solana users evaluate tokenized private-market exposure before they trade.

## Problem

Tokenized private-company exposure is new, exciting, and risky. Users need a way to compare assets, understand narrative and liquidity risk, simulate allocation size, and share a clear thesis without leaving their research workflow.

## Solution

Stockana gives users a compact research workflow: browse live PreStocks assets, filter by sector, score opportunities, simulate a portfolio allocation, save a persistent watchlist, share a direct asset research link, and export a bull/bear thesis card.

## Product Direction

Stockana makes tokenized private-market exposure easier to understand. It turns PreStocks assets from a list of tickers into a workflow for discovery, sizing, and thesis sharing.

The next product step is a wallet-aware research layer that can route from asset analysis into a Solana transaction flow when the user is ready to act.
