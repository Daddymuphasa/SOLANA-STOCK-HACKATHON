# StockLana Scout Submission Draft

## One-liner

StockLana Scout is a PreStocks discovery and portfolio simulation terminal that helps Solana users evaluate tokenized pre-IPO exposure before they trade.

## Track focus

Primary: PreStocks Best Integration.

Secondary: Stocklana Main Track.

## Problem

Tokenized private-company exposure is new, exciting, and risky. Users need a way to compare assets, understand narrative and liquidity risk, simulate allocation size, and share a clear thesis without leaving the Solana stock ecosystem.

## Solution

Scout gives users a compact research workflow:

- browse live PreStocks assets through the `/api/prestocks` adapter
- search and filter by sector
- score opportunities by liquidity, narrative strength, markup discipline, and risk fit
- simulate a portfolio allocation
- save a persistent local watchlist
- share a direct asset research link
- export a bull/bear thesis card
- explore the product through an interactive liquid-metal Three.js interface

## Demo script

1. Open the dashboard and show the PreStocks universe.
2. Search for an AI asset such as OpenAI or Anthropic.
3. Adjust risk appetite and show the score/allocation changing.
4. Open the research memo and show the real PreStocks product route and SPL mint.
5. Save the asset to the watchlist and copy a shareable asset URL.
6. Export the thesis and explain how the next version routes into a Solana swap flow.
