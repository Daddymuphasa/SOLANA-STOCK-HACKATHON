# Stockana Submission

## Project

Stockana is a research workspace for tokenized private-market assets on Solana. It connects to live PreStocks product data, turns the asset universe into a professional screener, and gives users an asset dossier with scoring, daily reference charts, SPL mint details, thesis notes, watchlists, and shareable research links.

## Track

PreStocks integration.

## What to submit

GitHub repository: `https://github.com/Daddymuphasa/SOLANA-STOCK-HACKATHON`

Live demo: add the Railway public URL after deployment.

Video walkthrough: record a 2-3 minute walkthrough showing the live product data, asset screener, right-side asset dossier, daily chart, watchlist, share link, thesis export, and PreStocks profile link.

## Demo flow

1. Open Stockana and show that live PreStocks data is loaded.
2. Search for `OpenAI` or `Anthropic`.
3. Click an asset in the market screener.
4. Walk through the asset dossier: score, reference price, suggested sleeve, daily reference chart, metrics, investment case, risk notes, SPL mint, and PreStocks profile link.
5. Save the asset to the watchlist.
6. Copy a shareable asset link.
7. Export the thesis.

## Short pitch

Stockana makes tokenized private-market exposure easier to research. PreStocks gives users access to private-market assets on Solana; Stockana adds the decision layer: discovery, risk scoring, reference charts, watchlists, thesis export, and direct links back to the PreStocks asset profile.
